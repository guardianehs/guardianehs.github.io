Developers

Open

Front-end, Postgres or applied-ML people who want a small, readable codebase with no build step and a clear purpose. The whole app is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

How the work is shared. Anything that touches how a permit or audit behaves goes past a practising EHS professional before it ships — a plausible-looking safety form is more dangerous than none. Anything that touches data goes through the database policies, not the interface.

If you want to help, the fastest route is to use Guardian on a real site for a week and send back the three things that annoyed you.
